⚡ How to Use the Habit Tracker

01

Add Custom Habits & Default Goal (Days)

Click "+ Add Habit" to create a new habit with your choice of emoji, name, and category. The Target Goal automatically defaults to the active month's total days (e.g. 31 days in 31-day months), or you can adjust it to any custom goal (e.g. 20 days for weekdays). You can also click "Presets" to load instant routines!

02

Track Habits & Instant Undo / Redo

Check off completed habits in the 31-Day Matrix. Use "✓ Check Today" for instant full completion, or use the Undo / Redo buttons (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) anytime to reverse or restore any mistake!

03

Watch Live Analytics & Streaks

Your performance updates in real-time across the Daily & Weekly Progress Bars, circular Donut % Gauge, and Goal / Completed / Left rollups. View your most consistent routines in the Top Habits Leaderboard.

04

Log Daily Wellness (Mood & Sleep)

Under the habit matrix, enter your Mood (1 to 5) and Hours of Sleep (e.g. 7.5h). The interactive Wellness Trend Graph correlates your sleep duration with daily mood and energy patterns.

05

Seamless Multi-Year & Month Navigation

Click any month tab (Jan – Dec) at the bottom or select any year from 2026 to 2100. Each month dynamically computes exact days (28–31) and calendar day-of-week headers (Su, Mo, Tu, We, Th, Fr, Sa).

06

Export & Print PDF / CSV

Click "Export" to download high-resolution printable PDF reports of your monthly dashboard, or export all data to CSV for spreadsheet analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.

💡 Pro-Tip: All progress is automatically saved and cloud-synced in real-time. Simply sign in with your Habit OS account to access your habits across all your devices!
